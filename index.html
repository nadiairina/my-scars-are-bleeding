My Scars
Are Bleeding

Poems by Nadia Irina

A collection of 76 poems in English, in four parts, from the first spark to the shadow that stays.

Request the manuscriptRead a few lines

One love, and what it leaves behind.

My Scars Are Bleeding follows one love from its first night to the long quiet after. The poems are short and plain, built on repetition and on the things a body remembers: blood, heart, night, absence.

Love, desire, abandonment, grief, depression and memory run through all four parts, so the book reads as one continuous story as well as 76 separate poems.

“An honest, disarming voice, writing from a place of total exposure.”

Nadia Irina is a web designer and poet based in Setúbal, Portugal. Her writing is a raw and honest meditation on extreme sensitivity and how it amplifies suffering. Her poetry transforms romantic and familial grief into verse, exploring the wounds that persist.

“I’ve always felt ‘too much’, every emotion feels like it might swallow me, and I struggle to understand and talk about it. […] I love creating in any shape or form.”

Nadia Irina, from the note at the end of the book
